# FlowDump

Talk, don't type. Hold your [Wispr Flow](https://wisprflow.ai) hotkey and ramble everything on your mind — FlowDump splits the dictated text into tasks and sorts them into **Today**, **This week** and **Later**, flagging anything urgent.

Built for the Wispr Flow task at Hacker House Goa 2026.

## How it works
1. Open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Click the box, hold your Wispr Flow hotkey and speak.
3. Tasks are split on sentences and connectors ("also", "and then", "plus"), then bucketed by time words (today, tomorrow, weekdays, "by Friday"…). Words like "urgent" or "asap" add a flag.

Single HTML file, no build step, no dependencies.
